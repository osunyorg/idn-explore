From "What's wrong with endless economic growth?" to " How will degrowth solve unemployment?" and everything in between.

body: >-


This page links to comprehensive Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs) resources from three leading organisations in the degrowth movement: Ontrgoei , the Dutch Degrowth Network, Associazione Per La Decrescita ,  based in Italy and NaZemi from the Czech Republic. Together, these groups have written over 50 FAQs meticulously organised into categories that cover a wide spectrum of topics. Each question is not only answered with insightful information but is also supported by sources for deeper exploration. Navigate through the topics to find answers, gain new perspectives, and enrich your knowledge.


The Ontrgoei FAQ page offers a detailed exploration of degrowth, available in both Dutch and English. Covering seven key topics, it provides both concise summaries and in-depth responses to each of the frequently asked questions, ensuring accessibility for readers seeking quick insights or thorough understanding. Each answer is thoughtfully cited, allowing for further investigation into the topics discussed. Engaging and informative, this resource is a valuable starting point for anyone curious about the principles and practices of degrowth.

This resource serves as an excellent guide for Italian-speaking audiences and those interested in global perspectives on degrowth, ensuring a broad understanding of its concepts and applications.
