We facilitate collaborative decision making, active and meaningful participation and challenge hierarchical power dynamics

body: >-


Transformative learning for degrowth promotes inclusive participation, valuing diverse perspectives and contributions from all groups, identities, and experiences. It creates learning spaces and community decision-making processes where voices from all social, age, racial, gender, and economic backgrounds are heard and respected. For example, participatory collaborative projects that empower marginalized or underrepresented groups, while incorporating environmental justice considerations, can challenge existing power structures and reveal systems of oppression that perpetuate such unequal power dynamics. This approach fosters a more equitable approach to sustainability and recognizes that everyone is an active contributor in co-creating more just and sustainable realities, rejecting top-down, one-size-fits-all solutions.

Metaphor: Beehive 🐝

Transformative Learning is a beehive, where every bee contributes to building, maintaining, and thriving together-each decision guided by collective wisdom rather than a single ruler.
