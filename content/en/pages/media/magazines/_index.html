Welcome to our dedicated space for exploring the transformative world of degrowth through our curated selection of magazines. Here, you’ll find insightful publications that delve deep into the principles of degrowth, featuring thought-provoking articles, in-depth analyses, and inspiring stories from around the globe. Whether you’re a long-time advocate or new to the concept, our collection of degrowth magazines serves as a vital resource to understand and engage with this crucial movement towards a more equitable and sustainable future.

15/15\15

15/15\15 Magazine is a pivotal voice for the degrowth movement in Spain
and Portugal, urging a shift toward sustainable living within ecological
limits. It uniquely involves its subscribers in shaping its direction,
ensuring it remains a grassroots, community-driven publication. Covering
topics from radical ecology to peak oil, the magazine fosters a
critical dialogue on constructing a sustainable future.

Check them out here!
https://www.15-15-15.org/webzine/en/

Caracol

Caracol is a zine produced by the Post-Growth forum, a student collective formed at Sciences Po Paris for awareness-raising and deliberation about building post-growth futures. The zine is a collaborative project envisioned as a space to question capitalism and explore radical alternatives in a collective and creative way. Credits: written and designed by María Quiñones Velasco, Oscar Fraser Turner and Katharina Frowein; edited by Milena Hanenberg and the Post-Growth Forum.

Journal de la Décroissance

Journal de la Décroissance is a critical and engaging publication
that promotes the principles of degrowth by challenging the
conventional narratives of economic growth and consumerism. Known for
its satirical critiques, vibrant illustrations and advocacy for a
society that values well-being over wealth, this French journal blends
academic insights with practical activism, encouraging a shift toward
sustainable and equitable lifestyles.

Check them out here!
